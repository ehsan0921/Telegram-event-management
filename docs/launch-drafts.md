## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: XEvents – open-source event management inside Telegram

I built XEvents for organising events where the community already chats: Telegram. Hosts can create events, send personal RSVP invitations or share ticket-booking links, track group attendance, check guests in with short-lived codes or optional QR tickets, and collect shared event media.

It uses JavaScript, a plain HTML/CSS Mini App, Cloudflare Workers and D1, under MIT. You can self-host it. Paid events support manual payment review and Telegram Stars; organiser payouts are manual, and hosting limits still apply.

Code: https://github.com/ehsan0921/Telegram-event-management
Try it: https://t.me/XEvents_bot

I'd welcome feedback on the host and guest flows, and on the self-hosting documentation.
